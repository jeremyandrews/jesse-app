# Workout feed inventory: what HealthKit holds that the feed does not read

This lists every workout relevant item that HealthKit can hold for a workout and that the per turn "recent workouts" feed (`HealthContextProvider` and `WorkoutContextFormatter`) still does not read. Source of truth is the installed iOS 26.2 SDK headers (`HKMetadata.h`, `HKMetadataEnums.h`, `HKTypeIdentifiers.h`, `HKWorkout.h`, `HKWorkoutActivity.h`, `HKWorkoutConfiguration.h`, `HKWorkoutRoute.h`, `HKWorkoutEffortRelationshipQuery.h`). Written 2026-10-05.

Treated as already covered, and so not listed: workout statistics for active energy, heart rate (average and maximum) and swimming, walking/running and cycling distance; source name and product type; the metadata keys IndoorWorkout, AverageMETs, ElevationAscended, ElevationDescended, WeatherTemperature, WeatherHumidity, WeatherCondition, WorkoutBrandName, LapLength, SwimmingLocationType, and SwimmingStrokeStyle plus SWOLFScore on lap events; lap events (swims, plus a lap count for runs, walks and hikes); duration and elapsed time; running power, ground contact time, vertical oscillation and stride length; stroke count, water temperature, step count and per km splits; rated and estimated effort through `predicateForWorkoutEffortSamplesRelated`; and route locations reduced to ascent, descent, minimum and maximum altitude.

Budget context: the whole health block is capped at 4096 bytes across a daily summary and up to five workout lines of roughly 300 to 500 bytes each, and segments shed from the right. Every derived value carries a basis marker such as `(computed)`. No coordinates or place names may ever leave the phone. Byte figures below include the leading `, ` separator. "Likely recorder" is a judgment, not a header fact: the headers say what a key means and where it may be set, never who writes it. Entries marked [guess] have no evidence beyond general knowledge of the platform and should be checked against a real export before code depends on them.

| Time in heart rate zones | Computed from `heartRate` samples over the workout window | Minutes spent in each of five HR bands. | `heartRate` minimum statistic | Workout statistic | `minimumQuantity()` of the HR statistics already fetched | Both | `min HR 92 bpm` | ~15 | Skip. Rarely meaningful during exercise; zones carry the distribution better |

## Add next

1. **Time in heart rate zones** (computed from the workout's HR samples, zone basis named in the marker). About 55 bytes.
2. **Heart rate recovery linked to the workout** (`heartRateRecoveryOneMinute` matched by its recovery metadata and time). About 25 bytes.
3. **Distance for the remaining activity types** (rowing, paddle, downhill and cross country skiing, skating, wheelchair statistics). Fills an existing field.
4. **Interval and multisport structure** from `workoutActivities` and `.segment` events, after a one time probe of a real interval session. About 45 to 80 bytes, shed before the detail segment.
5. **User entered flag** (`HKMetadataKeyWasUserEntered`). About 15 bytes.

## Excluded as not workout relevant

Metadata keys and types for food, insulin delivery, blood glucose meal time, lab reference ranges, ECG algorithm version, glasses prescription, sexual activity protection, menstrual cycle, audio and headphone exposure and gain, heart rate event thresholds, low cardio fitness thresholds, body temperature sensor location, device placement side, maximum light intensity and time in daylight, and all dietary, body measurement, respiratory function and reproductive quantity types were left out as not workout relevant.
